Each tile has a name on the screen (Home Assistant's unless one is typed) and a size in the screen's cells, across × down. Auto is one cell, or two across when its value is too long for one. They go across then down, each in the first place it fits. Tap a light or switch to switch it; hold a light to set its brightness. With more than one page, the screen's top bar has a tab for each, and a swipe across changes the page.
