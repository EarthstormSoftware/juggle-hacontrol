How tiles work

A tile's size is in the screen's cells, across × down; Auto is one cell, or two across when its value is too long for one. They go across then down, each in the first place it fits. A sensor can show its value as a gauge (along its kind's range unless one is given) or with a graph of its history. On the screen, tap a light or switch to switch it; hold a light for its brightness, warmth, colour and effects (whichever it has), or a sensor for its history. With more than one page, the top bar has a tab for each, and a swipe across changes the page.
